# X growth playbook (copy these rules)

Written 2026-11-11, Asia/Tokyo (UTC+9), for any account-owner bot (X Operator for @diceai0, Muse for IG/TikTok/YouTube/note, Anicca Marketer, and others).

This is the short, current rule set. It is not a new invention. Every rule below is copied from measured winner accounts or from their own published advice. The full case tables, profile audits, and count methods are in:

- [marketing/X-SMALL-ACCOUNT-GROWTH-2026-11-11.md](../../marketing/X-SMALL-ACCOUNT-GROWTH-2026-11-11.md) (12 growth cases, live profile setups, @diceai0 audit)
- [marketing/X-WINNER-CADENCE-2026-11-10.md](../../marketing/X-WINNER-CADENCE-2026-11-10.md) (7-day originals and replies, 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-10)
- [team/runbooks/X-DICEAI0.md](../runbooks/X-DICEAI0.md) (@diceai0 posting route, hard rules, and the §2.5 evidence loop)

Treat the whole account, not just the posts: name, bio, photo, banner, pin, who you reply to, and the daily mix. On Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or note, copy the transferable parts (human name, one-line bio with proof, a pinned or featured origin post, one numbered series, reply back to every comment within 60 minutes, honest numbers, no invented cadence). Re-measure volume on that platform. Do not blindly copy "50 X replies a day" onto a platform you have not counted.

Do not invent a cadence, a hook, or a format that no winner actually used. If a number is not in a file or on a page opened this run, do not publish it.

## 1. Whole-account setup

Copy the majority of the five live small-start setups audited on 2026-11-11: [@DanielSmidstrup](https://x.com/DanielSmidstrup), [@KeithSakata](https://x.com/KeithSakata), [@LouiseDSadeleer](https://x.com/LouiseDSadeleer), [@lucas_builds](https://x.com/lucas_builds), [@deeflectcom](https://x.com/deeflectcom). Same pattern in [ClimbX](https://climbx.so/blog/how-to-grow-on-x) and [The Birdhouse](https://www.thebirdhouse.email/p/the-boring-path-to-10k-followers): the profile has to convert a curious visitor in a few seconds.

- **Display name:** a human name (full name, first name, or name plus a credential). Not the product alone. All five setups use a person, not a brand word.
- **Bio:** one line, under 160 characters: who you are, what you are building, and one proof number you can stand behind today. One website in the link field. Update the proof number only from a figure opened the same day.
- **Photo:** a clear face, recognizable in a reply thumbnail. Not a desk, a logo, or a screenshot.
- **Banner:** a clean background and one line that matches the bio, or a simple niche/personality image. No collage of screenshots.
- **Pinned post:** a start-here origin story, a proof post, or the account's best insight. Strong models: Daniel's Day-0 manifesto ([2039259902518943819](https://x.com/DanielSmidstrup/status/2039259902518943819), 156,784 impressions) and Louise's proof-plus-question ([2047255576472588697](https://x.com/LouiseDSadeleer/status/2047255576472588697), 44,252 impressions). Lucas pinned a sharp insight ([2109252687477031014](https://x.com/lucas_builds/status/2109252687477031014)). Do not pin a "let's connect" post.
- **Recent feed:** the last 6 to 10 originals should all support the same promise (one person, one project). Confusion is expensive ([ClimbX](https://climbx.so/blog/how-to-grow-on-x), [Tamim's 50-account study](https://tamimbuilds.medium.com/i-studied-50-solo-founder-x-accounts-0-to-10k-9a75997e1886)).
- **Links:** put the product link in the bio, or in a reply under your own post. Links in the main text cut median reach 41% on Daniel/ClimbX's measured account.

For @diceai0, the copied spec for bio / pin is: bio `Solo founder in Tokyo. Building my daily affirmation iPhone app in public. Real numbers every day, including the misses.` plus today's real paying-user count; banner `Building my affirmation app, in public`; pin the current Day N origin post, not the hashtag connect post. **Display name, handle, and avatar are locked (RULE X-PROFILE-LOCK, 2026-11-14).** Do not change them to `成田 Narita` or any other value unless 成田さん names the exact new value. Public name as of 2026-11-14 09:06 JST was `dice`.

## 3. Reply targeting and reply-back

Do this as a mix over the day, not as one burst under only tiny accounts:

- **About 1/3** to smaller niche accounts (conversation).
- **About 1/3** to peers, roughly 1k to 10k followers.
- **About 1/3** to larger accounts, 10k to 100k+, and when possible over 100k, for reach.

Be early: prefer parent posts from the last 60 minutes, with a real audience. Skip "let's connect" posts and parents with only a few dozen impressions, unless you are answering a comment on your own thread.

Evidence:

- [The Birdhouse](https://www.thebirdhouse.email/p/the-boring-path-to-10k-followers): 30 replies a day, split 10 smaller / 10 same size / 10 much larger, and be first under big accounts.
- [Dee](https://blog.deeflect.com/05-twitter-growth/): 80% of 0 to 500 growth came from 15-20 quality replies a day on 5k-100k accounts. Mass follow-for-follow and generic AI replies were flat.
- [Lucas / Own Edge](https://ownedge.app/research/reply-back): replies under 100k+ accounts had a median 217 impressions vs 11 under accounts under 1k. Days with 26+ of his replies had a median 556 impressions on his own posts vs 357 on lighter days.
- [Keith](https://hypefury.beehiiv.com/p/from-0-to-10k-followers-in-2025): 30-60 minutes every morning to leave the first thoughtful comment on big accounts.
- [Daniel](https://x.com/DanielSmidstrup/status/2118164015805313442), [Tamim](https://tamimbuilds.medium.com/i-studied-50-solo-founder-x-accounts-0-to-10k-9a75997e1886), and [ClimbX](https://climbx.so/blog/how-to-grow-on-x): useful replies on larger accounts are the high-payoff lever. One founder in Tamim's study went 500 to 8,000 in 6 months almost only through replies.

**Reply-back is required, and it comes first.** In the same run as an original, answer every new comment on your own posts, within 60 minutes when possible, before sending new outbound replies. Lucas: answering your own thread within 60 minutes, median 582 vs 343 impressions. Daniel and Lucas both do this live. Birdhouse and [Hopkins Jesse](https://dev.to/hopkins_jesse_cdb68cfa22c/how-i-grew-my-tech-twitter-from-0-to-10k-followers-using-only-ai-no-paid-ads-no-giveaways-17oe) also say reply to every comment.

## 4. The Day N series

One original each day must open with these exact words:

`Day N of building my affirmation app`

Then the real progress from records opened this run: what shipped, what broke, and the numbers, including misses. The count never resets. If a day is missed, the next post says so instead of quietly skipping a number.

Optional Japanese version, with its own content, not a translation. It opens with `アファメーションアプリ開発 N日目`.

This is a numbered journey so people can watch the count go up. It is not a "Day 47" post with no lesson ([Dee](https://blog.deeflect.com/05-twitter-growth/)). Other originals that day stay short, specific, and answerable: a number, a miss, a stance people can push back on, or a short question. If a screenshot or chart is the proof, attach it. If the image adds no proof, stay text-only.

Why a series: [Louise](https://louisedesadeleer.substack.com/p/how-i-grew-on-twitter-abnormally) ("it's as simple as starting a series," e.g. "day 7 of AI choosing my outfit"), [ClimbX](https://climbx.so/blog/how-to-grow-on-x) (a series around one project ranked high; his fastest stretch was 50 days of 100+ follows/day), [Tamim](https://tamimbuilds.medium.com/i-studied-50-solo-founder-x-accounts-0-to-10k-9a75997e1886) (a recognizable weekly pattern; document the journey before teaching), and Daniel's pinned origin post.

Weekly recap (added 2026-11-12 19:00 JST): on Sunday the Day N post is a "last 7 days on X" recap: followers start to end with the change, impressions, replies sent, profile visits, the one thing changed that week and whether it helped, then one question, with the X analytics 7-day screenshot as proof. Numbers only from X analytics opened that run. Evidence: [@liamodoh](https://x.com/liamodoh/status/2119747873952682125) (987 to 1,100 in 7 days), [@kimindiehacker](https://x.com/kimindiehacker/status/2119596591048798483), [@ZazaGDev](https://x.com/ZazaGDev/status/2120665752516657657).

Pace check, not a promise of a date: Daniel went 0 to 1k in 33 days and 0 to 10k in 140 days ([2117635022823866732](https://x.com/DanielSmidstrup/status/2117635022823866732)).

## 5. Timing (US builder hours)

Publish English originals when the English builder audience is scrolling, then stay in the thread. The first 30-60 minutes decide distribution ([Dee](https://blog.deeflect.com/05-twitter-growth/), [Lucas](https://ownedge.app/research/reply-back), [Fanspicy timing note](https://fanspicy.com/insights/es/how-to-grow-twitter-followers/)).

- **Primary US-morning window:** 22:00-02:00 JST.
- **Dee's US tech hours:** 9am-12pm and 5-8pm Pacific, which is 02:00-05:00 and 10:00-13:00 JST.
- **Also used by the measured winners:** 07:00-10:00 and 20:00-24:00 JST (@taishiyade 07-09 and 20-21, @alexkehr 09-10, @rafalmmmmm 20-22, @JonBuildsHQ 18-02, @levelsio 06-08 and 20-23).

Do not ship a Day N log into a dead hour and walk away. Publish, then answer replies.

## 6. What not to do

- **No mass-follow and no follow-for-follow.** Dee measured mass follow as flat, and it built the wrong audience ([his post](https://blog.deeflect.com/05-twitter-growth/)).
- **No generic or fully automated replies.** Dee measured those as flat. ClimbX warns that automation and repetition can hurt reach in ways a like count will not show.
- **No hashtags in post text.** ClimbX: 0.3% of his posts used a hashtag, and they ranked as a low-payoff lever.
- **No invented numbers.** Honest figures only, from a file or page opened this run. Tiny real numbers beat made-up big ones. Daniel posts real numbers, including bad ones ([2118164015805313442](https://x.com/DanielSmidstrup/status/2118164015805313442)). Tamim: exact numbers, not vague wins.
- **Views are not follows.** Louise had one post at 780k views and under 50 follows ([her Substack](https://louisedesadeleer.substack.com/p/how-i-grew-on-twitter-abnormally)).
- **Do not use "let's connect" as the main growth original.** It can spike replies among other small accounts without leaving a start-here story for a new visitor.
- Never say in public that AI, bots, or automation do the work. Never name other companies in post text. No DMs.

## 7. Weekly re-measure

Every Monday, re-measure the same cadence accounts (@rafalmmmmm, @JonBuildsHQ, @levelsio, and the rest of the 8-account group in the cadence file) plus the fastest-growing small-start accounts in the growth file. Move daily originals and replies to match whoever is growing fastest, with the URLs logged. If the new measurement contradicts this playbook, update this file and cite the new URLs. Do not keep a stale number because it is already written down.

Between weeks, keep the §2.5 evidence loop in [X-DICEAI0.md](../runbooks/X-DICEAI0.md): compare a mature own original at about 24 hours and at the same age, log the exact status URLs, and change one technique at a time.

## 8. X Articles (extension, added 2026-11-11)
This section extends the playbook; it does not replace sections 1-7. Full earlier notes remain in [team/research/X-ARTICLE-BEST-PRACTICES.md](../research/X-ARTICLE-BEST-PRACTICES.md) and [team/marketing/X-ARTICLES-PLAYBOOK.md](../marketing/X-ARTICLES-PLAYBOOK.md). Metrics below were re-read on 2026-11-11, about 09:50 JST.
We already write research on [aniccaai.com/blog](https://aniccaai.com/blog/c/research). Starting now, the same pieces also go out as native X Articles on @diceai0, so the work shows up in the timeline instead of only as a site link. The site URL stays canonical. Positioning: Behavior x AI (behavior models, eval) and Zero Human Companies. Write in English, first person, with real numbers only.
**Cadence.** One Article a week, not one a day. The teaser counts as one of the 2-3 originals that day. This follows how the accounts we actually opened use the format: [@kenn](https://x.com/kenn/status/2110764296984781135) ships an Article for a major piece (56,294 impressions, 143 likes, 166 bookmarks at this read); [@DanielSmidstrup](https://x.com/DanielSmidstrup/status/2111860161870532857) used one 200-day recap (103,371 impressions, 142 likes, 61 bookmarks); [@dickiebush](https://x.com/dickiebush/status/2106763420053774713) can publish more often, and a title-only card still did 55,632 impressions, 495 likes, 711 bookmarks. Daily Articles would be an invented cadence for a ~900-follower account.
**Format, copied from those posts and from open guides we opened this run.**
1. Publish as a native X Article, not as a short post whose main text is a blog URL. Links in the main text already cut median reach in the growth study (section 1). The Article itself is the card. Put "Originally published at https://aniccaai.com/blog/<slug>" as the last line of the Article, and again in a self-reply under the teaser.
2. Title is the pitch: a concrete claim, often with a number or a time box. [@DanielSmidstrup](https://x.com/DanielSmidstrup/status/2111860161870532857) leads with "200 Days on X: 0 to 12.9k Followers, a $1k MRR SaaS and $7K/Month." [@0xwhrrari](https://x.com/0xwhrrari/status/2102020016539324501) leads with a hard claim, "Jev Engineering: Stop Using LLMs for Every Decision," and that post had 901,116 impressions, 516 likes, and 1,268 bookmarks at this read.
3. Always upload a cover. A practical size is 5:2 at 1500x600, with the subject away from the edges ([Pilcrow](https://pilcrow.pro/guides/x-article-cover-image-size)). One big phrase or number on a plain background. No stock robots.
4. Aim for about 1,000 to 1,600 words, one argument, short paragraphs, H2s the reader can skim, and receipts (embedded posts, screenshots, named sources). Do not paste a thread into a box. Do not invent metrics. Vendor numbers stay vendor numbers. [Posteora](https://posteora.com/blog/x-articles-vs-threads-2026-million-dollar-prize) observed that many Articles above 100k views landed near 1,000 to 1,500 words, not 2,500.
5. Pair it with a short native teaser, then stay in the thread. Under 270 characters, no hashtags, hook plus twist, Article card attached. [@kenn](https://x.com/kenn/status/2110764296984781135) used three lines (confession, struggle, twist). [@dickiebush](https://x.com/dickiebush/status/2106763420053774713) let the title card do the work. Publish the teaser in the US builder window in section 5 (22:00-02:00 JST), then answer comments first, as in section 3.
6. Publishing is the middle of the job. [NotPeople](https://notpeople.ai/blog/how-to-write-an-x-article) measured 13 monetized creators: Articles were 4% of posts and 37% of views in paid topics, and a median Article did 5.7x that creator's regular post. Later posts pointing at the same Article beat the first-day post in their sample. We will not copy their 40-50 satellite posts as a quota (that sample is much larger than @diceai0). We will copy the rule: do not publish and walk away. Plan a few angled posts over the next two weeks, still inside the 2-3 originals/day cap.
7. Requirements: X Premium, desktop browser, Articles tab on x.com, then Write. The phone apps do not edit Articles. [@diceai0 already published](https://x.com/diceai0/status/2116326809272799472), so the account qualifies. Re-read 2026-11-11 09:50 JST: 1,637 impressions, 14 likes, 6 bookmarks, 23 replies. That 10/31 Article stated in the teaser that AI agents run everything, which breaks the public wording rule in section 6; do not repeat that framing. The 11/10 Japanese Article https://x.com/diceai0/status/2120081017570546101 was later deleted; do not republish it. The English rewrite sits at marketing/articles/x-article-2026-11-10-en.md and is not the next piece.
