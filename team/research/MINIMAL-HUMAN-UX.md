## 3. Stack-up rule (成田さん 09:35 JST correction)


Show them as a **growing stack**, sorted by pay, with urgent deadlines flagged. Psychology: a big stack of ready opportunities makes people think "let me try one." Most people will sit for about a week and then try one item — the stack is what makes that one item happen.

Each line must include:

- pay
- what to do
- minutes needed
- deadline (flag URGENT when close)
- a link that works whenever it is opened (no login wall, never stale)

If a link expires or starts asking for login, re-fetch a fresh working link; do not resend an old one. **Never include login, Gmail, phone, or "your own browser" steps** in the stack. Items that are not fully prepared stay in the agent-owned queue until they are.
