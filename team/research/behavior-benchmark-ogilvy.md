## Case studies with measured results (quoted as published)

### 1. “One Bin is Rubbish… Sort it Out!” (London recycling, 2017–2020)

Source: Ogilvy Behavioural Science Annual 2020, Dan Bennett, pp. 7–9 of the PDF above. Trial 1 Nov 2017 – 1 Feb 2020, 3 years.

- Published results: 137,299 Londoners who saw the campaign bought a new bin (8% of the target audience). Awareness of the issue 32% → 55%. Social norm of recycling 43% → 61%. Average bins per household 1.1 (2017) → 2.1 (2020).
- Principles in use: reframe the job (bin, not “try harder”), social norms, a specific ask, salient London media (red buses).

### 2. Adobe: “Rewriting the Script to Overcome Churn” (UK, 2023 Annual)

Source: *The Behavioral Science Annual 2023*, Mike Hughes, “Adobe: Rewriting the Script to Overcome Churn.” PDF pp. 20–21. https://www.ogilvy.com/sites/g/files/dhpsjz106/files/pdfdocuments/Ogilvy_The%20Behavioral%20Science%20Annual%202023.pdf

- Problem: subscription churn; monetary incentives were costly; analysis showed a value-perception problem.
- Intervention: stop asking “why are you cancelling?” (which primed confirmation of reasons to leave). Winning script primed the original choice: “Why did you choose Adobe in the first place?”
- Principle: priming + reframe of the conversation. No discount.

### 3. WRAP / Ramadan Rihla food-waste pilot (UK, 2023 Annual)

Source: same 2023 PDF, “Ramadan Rihla: Eat, Keep, Reheat, Repeat.”

- Intervention: a “good deeds” calendar framed as a *rihla* (journey), piloted with 106 families over Ramadan. Deeds were spiritually framed and only subtly aimed at food-waste behaviour; badges could be cashed for a prize.
- Published result: 67% of families reported a lot less, or a little less, food waste than normal. 72% of parents reported they had learnt something new. 60% agreed it had changed the way they thought about food.
- Principle: reframe (waste reduction as a good deed / journey), commitment, bundling with an existing ritual.

### 4. Costa Coffee cup recycling (2022 Annual, qualitative)

Source: *The Behavioural Science Annual 2022*, Rory Sutherland / practice retrospective: the team reframed “disposable cup” as “recyclable cup.” https://www.ogilvy.com/ideas/behavioral-science-annual-2022

- A published lift percentage for this Costa reframe was **not** in the pages we extracted. Do not invent one. Keep it as a principle example only.
