## 1. Patrick & John Collison (Stripe) — Irish outsiders
- Outsider start: Limerick, Ireland. Patrick won BT Young Scientist 2005; brothers sold first company Auctomatic for ~$5M. Source: https://www.irishtimes.com/business/technology/limerick-brothers-have-earned-their-stripe-1.9320 , https://www.siliconrepublic.com/start-ups/peter-thiel-invests-in-collison-brothers-latest-venture-stripe
- The move: **"Collison installation."** When anyone said "yes I'll try it", they said "Right then, give me your laptop" and set it up on the spot. Also: the "instant" merchant accounts were done manually by the founders behind the scenes. Source: Paul Graham, "Do Things That Don't Scale" https://paulgraham.com/ds.html
- First big names: 2011 seed from Peter Thiel and Elon Musk (+ Sequoia, a16z, SV Angel). Source: https://techcrunch.com/2011/03/28/stealth-payment-startup-stripe-paypal/
- Copy for us: never send a link. Install Anicca on the person's phone in front of them (in person or on a live call), and finish setup before they leave.

Source: Wired above, https://www.cnbc.com/make-it/2023/04/18/airbnb-ceo-says-he-wooed-first-investor-with-boxes-of-cereal.html

- Copy for us: (a) a physical/visible artifact that proves hustle and funds us (numbered limited edition), (b) one warm intro to the decision-maker beats the form, (c) go to the users' homes.

## The pattern (what all of them did that we are NOT doing enough)
1. **Go straight to the top name.** Son → Den Fujita / Jack Ma. Collisons → Thiel + Musk. Cursor → OpenAI. Airbnb → PG via Seibel. We have sent ~0 direct messages to top-50 people.
2. **Do the unscalable thing in person.** Collison install; Airbnb door to door; Musk sleeping in the office.
3. **A stunt/artifact that proves hustle and creates money** (cereal boxes, translator sold to Sharp).
4. **Biggest distribution partner early** (Loopt → Sprint; Zip2 → NYT; Son → Sharp).
5. **Public, loud, with numbers** (Levels, Lovable, Scale's Product Hunt + TechCrunch day).
6. **All-in reinvestment and speed** (Musk kept the chips on the table; Cursor doubled ARR every ~2 months).

## What we do NOT copy
- Lying about facts or eligibility (e.g. pretending to be a student). Every winner above got the big name through real proof + a real introduction; one exposed lie ends access to that network permanently. Ambition ≠ fraud.
- Saying publicly that AI agents do our work (company rule). Public copy talks about the product and the founder.
