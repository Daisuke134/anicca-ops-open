## 2. Benchmarks (with sources)
- RevenueCat SOSA 2025, Health & Fitness medians: **weekly $4.99, monthly $9.70, annual $39.99**. Trial→paid median is 39.9% (top 10%: 68.3%). https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/
- **Hard paywall: D35 download→paid median 12.11%, versus 2.18% for freemium.** 80–90% of trials start on Day 0, so the paywall belongs inside onboarding. (Same source; https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/sosa-2025-launch-sub-club)
- Longer trials convert better: 17–32 days gives 45.7%, while ≤4 days gives 26.8%. 3-day and 7-day trials have the highest day-0/1 cancellations. (SOSA 2025)
- Weekly plans: median $4.99, and weekly users pay about 3.2× more per month than monthly users. https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/weekly-subscriptions
- Category leaders:
  - Affirmations: I am – Daily Affirmations charges $59.99/yr (3-day trial), $14.99/mo and $149.99 lifetime. It raised its yearly price from $35.99 to $59.99 in Jul 2025. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/i-am-daily-affirmations/id874656917 , https://appslupa.com/app/i-am-daily-affirmations/id874656917
  - Peptalk charges $34.99–59.99/yr. https://www.mindfulsuite.com/reviews/best-affirmations-apps
  - Sleep/meditation: Calm is $79.99/yr or $16.99/mo with a 14-day trial; Headspace is $69.99/yr; BetterSleep is $59.99/yr. https://www.calm.com , https://www.mindkindly.com/articles/wellness-app-costs-2026
  - Gratitude: Five Minute Journal and Gratitude Plus are $39.99/yr; Day One is $49.99/yr. https://blog.mylifenote.ai/best-gratitude-journal-apps/
